## Important scope boundary

This is an educational lab PoC. It is not Qilin's original ransomware, does not show access to Asahi's production systems, and does not prove that the same payload or exact workflow was used in the real incident.

The video does not preserve the original VM CPU/RAM allocation, VMnet mode, IP addressing, VLAN IDs, firewall rules, central logging setup, or backup configuration. The original scripts and VM images were not recovered.

The video remains hosted at the original Google Drive link. Access depends on the Drive owner's sharing permission.

## Safety

Use only in a disposable, isolated lab that you own or are authorized to test. Do not execute payloads on production systems or third-party networks. Revert to a known-clean baseline after an exercise.
